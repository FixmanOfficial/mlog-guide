```
bullet shot @duo @graphite 5 5 0 @sharded null -1 1 1 0 0
```

Once a second a duo's graphite bullet flies out of tile five — although there is no turret on
the map at all. The dagger on the right loses health and dies after a dozen shots.

| Field | What is in it |
| --- | --- |
| first | where to put the created bullet |
| second | **whose bullet we borrow**: a turret or unit type |
| third | which ammo: an item for an item turret, a weapon index for a unit |
| fourth and fifth | where it flies from, in tiles |
| sixth | the angle in degrees |
| seventh | the team |
| eighth | the owner: the unit or building the shot is credited to |
| ninth | damage; `-1` means "as the ammo's" |
| tenth and eleventh | multipliers for speed and lifetime |
| twelfth and thirteenth | the aim point — for bullets that use one |

## A bullet always belongs to somebody

The instruction has no bullet kind of its own: it borrows a ready **type** from whoever fires
such bullets.

- from an item turret — by ammo: `@duo` plus `@copper`, `@graphite` or `@silicon`;
- from a power turret — its own shot, with no ammo needed;
- from a unit — by weapon index: `@dagger` and `0`.

Hence the strength of the shot: graphite from a duo hits for 18, copper for 9. The ninth field
lets you rewrite the damage without touching anything else.

<Card kind="note">
The team and the owner are different things. The team decides who gets hurt; the owner who the
hit is credited to. The owner can be a unit or a building, or it can be left empty: then the
shot simply has no author.
</Card>

## What it is for

- **weapons the game does not have**: traps, shooting walls, delayed mines;
- **scripted events**: shelling the base "from afar", a meteor shower;
- **spectacle**: a victory salute, tracer fire along a road.

<Card kind="error">
**A bullet checks nothing.** Not ammo, not reload, not firing range — as many as you say fly
out. A program calling `bullet` every iteration fires several hundred bullets a second and
brings the game down exactly as <Lesson lesson="spawn">`spawn`</Lesson> does without a limiter.

So shots are always paced: by a <Lesson lesson="advanced/time">timer</Lesson>, as in the
example, or by an event.
</Card>

<Card kind="note">
**The course sandbox does not support unit bullets** — only item turrets'. In the game the
instruction works as usual.
</Card>

<Card kind="recap">
- `bullet` creates a bullet at a point on the map, with no turret and no shooter.
- The bullet's type is borrowed from a turret or a unit; an item turret also needs ammo.
- The team decides who gets hurt; the owner who the shot is credited to.
- Damage, speed and lifetime can be rewritten with multipliers.
- The instruction checks neither ammo nor reload — the program keeps count.
</Card>

<Card kind="task">
Change the ammo in the example from `@graphite` to `@copper`: the damage halves and the dagger
lives noticeably longer.

Then set the ninth field to `100` instead of `-1`: the bullet looks the same but hits five
times harder than graphite.
</Card>
