`sensor` asks about a **particular** block or unit the program already holds in a variable.
A radar answers a different question: **who is nearby**.

```
radar enemy any any distance duo1 1 aim
```

It finds exactly one — the "best" by the chosen measure — and only a **unit**: a radar does not
see buildings at all.

<Card kind="note">
A radar has no range of its own: it looks as far as the block being asked can see. On a turret
that is its firing range, on a processor its link range.
</Card>
