`ulocate` answers the question "where is the nearest". It searches **from the bound unit**, and
what it finds is not units but places on the map: a tile with ore or a building.

Hence its shape: the answer is always coordinates, a "found" flag and — if a building is
involved — the building object itself.

<Card kind="note">
`ulocate`, like the orders, **takes the unit under control**: a controller is created and the
control term starts counting again. Searching the map is not a question from the sidelines but
part of working with the unit.
</Card>
