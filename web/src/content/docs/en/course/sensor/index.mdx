By itself a processor knows nothing but its own variables. Everything else it learns in
exactly one way — by asking. `sensor` is what asks.

The block has three fields: where to put the answer, what to ask, and who to ask. You can ask
a building, a unit, a block type and even an item — but not everything of everyone, and that
is the group's main subtlety.

The full text form and the list of 77 properties are in the
[reference](/en/reference/instructions/sensor/) and in the
[property table](/en/reference/properties/). Here is how to use it.

## What you need first

Only one thing: how a block comes to be linked to a processor. In the game you place links
with the mouse; in our examples they are placed already and are called `container1`, `vault1`,
`duo1` — exactly what you will see on your own base.
